## Position and boundary

The site should help homeowners, hospitality operators, landscape teams, designers, and procurement staff plan a timber gazebo from requirements through handover and maintenance. It owns wood selection, layout, structural interfaces, roof and water management, durability, finishes, build quality, use planning, and comparable procurement.

It does not provide structural calculations, permit determinations, electrical design, chemical-treatment recipes, universal span tables, unsupported prices, or city-swapped pages. Final dimensions, member sizes, connections, foundations, and wind/seismic resistance require project-specific design by competent parties.

## Existing-corpus and internal-link rules

1. Legacy city/material pages should be consolidated into material/service hubs where feasible; do not create new location-swapped articles.
2. GZK-01 is the decision hub and links to the timber, site, frame, roof, durability, and procurement clusters.
3. GZK-02 links forward to GZK-07 and GZK-08 because species choice alone does not determine outdoor durability.
4. GZK-03 links to GZK-04 through GZK-06 when layout choices change structure or water management.
5. GZK-09 receives build-stage links from every system topic and links to GZK-12 at handover.
6. GZK-11 may summarize scope dependencies but must link to technical owners rather than repeat their guidance.
7. Comparisons with bamboo, steel, or concrete stay in GZK-01; separate material clusters would dilute this timber-focused domain.

## Evidence gates

- Do not publish member dimensions, spans, footing sizes, anchor capacity, wind/seismic resistance, occupancy, or load ratings without project calculations and a responsible qualified designer.
- Wood species, grade, moisture content, treatment retention, legal origin, and chain-of-custody claims require supplier documentation and the relevant current standard.
- Treatment, coating, adhesive, sealant, preservative, and termite-control claims require named product technical/safety data and compatibility with the chosen timber.
- Service-life, maintenance-interval, warranty, lead-time, and price claims require documented project conditions and dated supplier/contractor evidence.
- Roof pitch, fastener spacing, flashing, and drainage details require the selected roof-system instructions and project rain/wind conditions.
- Site examples, quantities, defect diagnoses, and before/after photos require provenance, permission, and project context.
- Accessibility, permits, electrical work, fire safety, and hospitality/public-use requirements must be checked against the current local authority and owner brief.

Publish GZK-01, GZK-02, GZK-03, GZK-07, GZK-09, and GZK-11 first. This creates a selection-to-procurement path while structural, foundation, and roof articles wait for stronger project-specific engineering evidence.
